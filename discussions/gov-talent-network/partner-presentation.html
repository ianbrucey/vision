The Problem We Solve

The Government's Side

Federal agencies must award a share of contracts to small businesses. They want to — but the process defeats most small contractors before they even bid.

  • Registration, bonding, compliance
  • Proposal writing, past performance
  • Invoicing, milestone tracking

The Small Business Side

Most small contractors never bid on federal work. Not because they can't do the job — because the paperwork is the real barrier.

  • ~250 active SB construction set-asides this week alone
  • $100K–$150K+ opportunities: big enough to build on, small enough that large players don't compete

We remove the process. Subs do what they're great at. We do what they're not.

How It Works — The Three Roles

🏛️

We (The Prime)

Hold the contract. Face the agency. Own the compliance and paperwork. Manage to completion.

Financial & Bonding Strategy

Under $150,000

The Simplified Acquisition Threshold — no bid bond or performance bond required.

  • Focus construction opportunities here initially
  • Low barrier to entry
  • Build past performance while minimizing risk

Over $150,000

Partner or piggyback with established construction companies that already have:

  • Strong bonding capacity
  • Past performance records
  • We bring the deal flow — they bring the credentials

Revenue Model

We earn the full contract value as prime. Our margin is the difference between the price we win and the prices our subs quote. Scale comes from software and network, not headcount.

What's Already Built

SAM.gov Pipeline ✅

  • CSV upload: 46K rows in ~2 seconds
  • Five-gate filter: type → NAICS → set-aside → urgency → dedup
  • Row-level idempotency: same CSV uploaded twice = zero duplicates
  • One-click "Filter → Solicitations": creates solicitation records for all qualifying notices
  • AI triage pipeline wired and ready (sam_fetch → triage → vendor matching)
  • Clickable notice IDs resolve to SAM.gov URLs

USASpending Pipeline ✅

  • 136K-row CSV upload with streaming (no memory issues)
  • Four-gate filter: plan F/G → active ordering → NAICS → valid keys
  • Priority scoring: 0–100 based on plan type, value, pool membership
  • Multi-award pool identification: groups primes by solicitation
  • Dedup via (award_id_piid, recipient_uei)
  • Expandable row detail: agency, NAICS, socioeconomic flags, permalink

Infrastructure

FastAPI backend · Next.js 16 frontend · PostgreSQL + pgvector · Claude AI integration · Background job queue · JWT auth · Mailgun email · SAM.gov API client · OCR pipeline for PDFs/DOCX

Get Paid On Time

We handle the government invoicing. Subs get paid when we get paid. No chasing agencies.

Key insight (Eric Coffie): "Stop trying to be the whole contract. Be a line item." A solo HVAC tech can join a maintenance contract. A one-truck cleaner can land a facilities contract line. Line items → cash flow → credibility → past performance → contracts.
