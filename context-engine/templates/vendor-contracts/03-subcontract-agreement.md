## ARTICLE 9 — TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination for Convenience (Government-Directed).** If the Government terminates the prime contract for convenience, Justice Quest may terminate this Subcontract for convenience. Justice Quest shall pay Subcontractor's allowable, allocable, and reasonable costs incurred through the termination date, plus a proportionate share of any termination settlement proceeds received from the Government for Subcontractor's work.

**9.2 Termination for Convenience (Justice Quest-Directed).** Justice Quest may terminate this Subcontract for convenience upon fifteen (15) days' written notice. Justice Quest shall pay Subcontractor's reasonable costs incurred through the termination date.

**9.3 Termination for Default.** Justice Quest may terminate this Subcontract for Subcontractor's default if Subcontractor: (a) fails to perform any material obligation and does not cure within ten (10) days of written notice; or (b) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy. Upon default termination, Subcontractor shall be liable for Justice Quest's reprocurement costs.
