## RECITALS

Justice Quest is a Georgia-based professional services firm that pursues federal and state government contracts as a prime contractor. Vendor possesses capabilities, past performance, and qualifications that complement Justice Quest's service offerings. The Parties desire to establish a framework governing their teaming relationship for the purpose of pursuing Government contract opportunities on the terms set forth herein.

---

## ARTICLE 1 — RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES

**1.1 No Joint Venture or Partnership.** This Agreement does not create a joint venture, partnership, employment relationship, or agency between the Parties. Neither Party has the authority to bind the other in any manner not expressly authorized herein.

**1.2 Prime Contractor Status.** Justice Quest shall act as the prime contractor on any awarded Government contract pursued under this Agreement. Vendor shall act solely as a subcontractor. Nothing herein obligates either Party to enter into any subcontract, bid on any specific opportunity, or award any work to Vendor.

**1.3 Independent Contractors.** Each Party is an independent contractor. Vendor's employees, agents, and subcontractors are not employees of Justice Quest and are not entitled to any benefits, workers' compensation, or employment protections from Justice Quest.

**1.4 SBA Affiliation Disclaimer.** The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement is structured to avoid a finding of affiliation under 13 CFR 121.103 (including the ostensible subcontractor rule at 13 CFR 121.103(h)(3)). Neither Party shall take any action under this Agreement that would cause the other to lose its independent small business status.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF MARKETING AUTHORIZATION

**2.1 Authorization Grant.** Vendor hereby grants Justice Quest a non-exclusive, revocable, royalty-free authorization to represent Vendor's capabilities, past performance records, certifications, personnel qualifications, equipment, bonding capacity, and NAICS codes in responses to Government solicitations, subject to the conditions in Section 2.2.

**2.2 Conditions on Use.** Justice Quest's use of Vendor's information under Section 2.1 is conditioned on:

(a) A Bid-Specific Teaming Addendum ("BSTA") in the form of Exhibit A having been fully executed by both Parties for the relevant solicitation prior to proposal/offer submission; and

(b) All representations made about Vendor are accurate as of the date of submission.

**2.3 Vendor Responsibility for Accuracy.** Vendor is solely responsible for ensuring that all information it provides to Justice Quest (capabilities, certifications, personnel qualifications, past performance, bonding capacity, size status) is current, accurate, and complete. Vendor shall update such information within five (5) business days of any material change.

**2.4 No Guarantee of Inclusion.** Execution of this Agreement and any BSTA does not guarantee that Justice Quest will include Vendor in any specific bid, select Vendor for any subcontract, or win any award.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — SIMILARLY SITUATED ENTITY REPRESENTATION

**3.1 Statutory Framework.** The Parties acknowledge that under FAR 52.219-14(b), a "similarly situated entity" means a subcontractor that: (i) has the same small business program status as that which qualified the prime for the award; and (ii) is small under the NAICS code assigned to the subcontract. Work performed by a similarly situated entity counts favorably toward the prime's self-performance percentage under the Limitations on Subcontracting (LoS) clause.

**3.2 Vendor Representation.** Vendor represents and warrants that, as of the Effective Date:

(a) Vendor qualifies as a small business concern under the size standards applicable to its primary NAICS code(s) as listed in SAM.gov;

(b) Vendor's SAM.gov registration is active, complete, and accurate; and

(c) Vendor holds the certifications, program designations, and socioeconomic status listed in its SAM.gov profile and in any documentation provided to Justice Quest.

**3.3 Per-Bid Re-Certification.** Vendor shall re-confirm its similarly situated status at the time each BSTA is executed. If Vendor's status, size, or certifications have changed since the prior representation, Vendor shall disclose such changes in writing before executing the BSTA.

**3.4 Notice of Status Change.** Vendor shall provide written notice to Justice Quest within five (5) business days of any change that would affect Vendor's small business status, program eligibility, SAM.gov registration, or certification status.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — WORKSHARE COMMITMENT FRAMEWORK

**4.1 Per-Bid Workshare.** For each opportunity covered by an executed BSTA, Vendor commits to perform the scope of work and workshare percentage specified in that BSTA.

**4.2 Prime's Certification Authority.** Vendor acknowledges that Justice Quest, as prime contractor, bears full responsibility for LoS compliance certifications made to the Government and retains sole discretion in determining and certifying self-performance percentages. Vendor shall not make independent representations to the Government regarding contract performance percentages without Justice Quest's prior written consent.

**4.3 Performance Backstop.** If Vendor fails to perform its committed workshare under an active BSTA or resulting subcontract, and such failure causes Justice Quest to breach its LoS obligations to the Government or exposes Justice Quest to liability under the False Claims Act (31 U.S.C. § 3729 et seq.), Vendor shall indemnify and hold Justice Quest harmless from all resulting damages, costs, fines, and reasonable attorneys' fees attributable to Vendor's failure.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — CONFIDENTIALITY

**5.1 Confidential Information.** Each Party ("Receiving Party") agrees to hold in strict confidence all non-public information disclosed by the other Party ("Disclosing Party") in connection with this Agreement, including: bid strategies, pricing approaches, solicitation analyses, teammate identities, and proprietary processes ("Confidential Information").

**5.2 Permitted Disclosures.** Confidential Information may be disclosed only to the Receiving Party's employees and contractors who have a need to know and are bound by obligations at least as protective as this Article.

**5.3 Exclusions.** Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was rightfully known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or Government regulation.

**5.4 Duration.** Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for three (3) years.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — PER-BID EXCLUSIVITY

**6.1 No Global Exclusivity.** This Agreement does not restrict Vendor from pursuing Government contracts independently or teaming with other prime contractors on opportunities not covered by an active BSTA.

**6.2 Per-Solicitation Exclusivity.** For each solicitation covered by an executed BSTA, Vendor agrees not to team with, support, or provide capabilities to any other prime contractor bidding on that same solicitation, for the duration of the BSTA's active period. This restriction is limited to the specific solicitation number identified in the BSTA.

**6.3 Conflict Disclosure.** If Vendor identifies a potential conflict with Section 6.2 with respect to any solicitation for which a BSTA is proposed, Vendor shall disclose the conflict to Justice Quest before executing the BSTA.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — FLOW-DOWN CLAUSES

**7.1 Vendor Acknowledgment.** Vendor acknowledges that any subcontract executed under an awarded Government prime contract will incorporate mandatory FAR and agency-specific flow-down clauses required by the prime contract. Vendor agrees to execute a subcontract containing such clauses as a condition of receiving work.

**7.2 Minimum Flow-Downs.** Without limitation, applicable subcontracts may include flow-downs of:

- FAR 52.222-26 (Equal Opportunity)
- FAR 52.222-21 (Prohibition of Segregated Facilities)
- FAR 52.203-7 (Anti-Kickback Procedures)
- FAR 52.215-2 (Audit and Records — if cost-reimbursement prime contract)
- FAR 52.219-14 (Limitations on Subcontracting — if above SAT)
- Any additional clauses required by the specific prime contract

---

## ARTICLE 8 — TERM AND TERMINATION

**8.1 Term.** This Agreement is effective from the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years, unless earlier terminated. The Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either Party provides thirty (30) days' written notice of non-renewal.

**8.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

**8.3 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other Party: (a) materially breaches and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days of notice; (b) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy; or (c) makes a material misrepresentation in connection with a Government bid or certification.

**8.4 Effect on Active BSTAs.** Termination of this Agreement does not affect any BSTA that is currently active. Active BSTAs continue in force until their natural conclusion or separate termination.

---

## ARTICLE 9 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**9.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, except that matters governed by federal law (including FAR and SBA regulations) are governed by applicable federal law.

**9.2 Dispute Resolution.** The Parties shall attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation for at least thirty (30) days before pursuing other remedies. Unresolved disputes shall be submitted to binding arbitration under AAA rules in Gwinnett County, Georgia.

**9.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all executed BSTAs and Subcontract Agreements, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations and agreements.

**9.4 Amendments.** No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

**9.5 Counterparts / Electronic Signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature (e.g., DocuSign), each of which is an original.
