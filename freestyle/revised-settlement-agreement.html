CONFIDENTIAL SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND RELEASE
This Confidential Settlement Agreement and Release (the "Agreement") is entered into between Ian Bruce (referred to as "Plaintiff"), on the one hand, and American Express National Bank ("Defendant" or "American Express"), on the other hand. Plaintiff and American Express are collectively referred to as the "Parties."
RECITALS
WHEREAS, Plaintiff is the Basic Card Member on American Express Hilton Honors Card Account ending in 5-61009, which is cancelled and closed and has an outstanding balance of $1,645.47 (the "Account").
WHEREAS, on or about July 2, 2026, Plaintiff filed suit in the Magistrate Court for Gwinnett County, Georgia, styled Ian Bruce v. American Express National Bank, Case No. 26-M- 32128, therein alleging violations of the Fair Credit Reporting Act, among other allegations, against American Express in connection with the Account (the "Action").
WHEREAS, American Express denies any liability in connection with the Action or any dispute with Plaintiff or associated with the Account.
WHEREAS, the Parties desire to fully resolve the underlying Action and any other claims, actions, disputes, controversies or legal causes of action that were asserted or that could have been asserted in connection with the Action and the Account.
NOW, THEREFORE, it is hereby agreed by and between the Parties that:
I. Effective Date
This Agreement will become effective when fully executed by all of the Parties ("Effective Date").
II. Consideration
A. Plaintiff.
1. Notice of Settlement and Dismissal with Prejudice of the Action: Plaintiff shall file with the Court in the Action a Notice of Settlement within five (5) days of the Effective Date. Plaintiff shall also file with the Court in the Action a Dismissal with Prejudice, and any other appropriate pleading(s)/paper(s) or stipulation as may be necessary to effectuate a full and complete resolution of the Action against American Express within five (5) days of Plaintiff's receipt of the Settlement Payment set forth in Paragraph II(B)(1). Plaintiff shall notify and/or provide American Express with a copy of said Dismissal with Prejudice to CCML@aexp.com. If an adverse order and/or judgment is entered by the Court against American Express after the execution of this Agreement, Plaintiff shall file any documents necessary for the Court to vacate any such order and/or judgment, and facilitate dismissal with prejudice of the Action.
2. Accounts: Plaintiff understands and acknowledges that this Agreement does not release it from its obligations with respect to any past, current, or future accounts that it may have with American Express.
3. Tax Information Reporting: Plaintiff acknowledges and agrees that American Express will report this settlement to the Internal Revenue Service as required by law. The Parties acknowledge and agree that the Settlement Payment of $1,000.00 referenced in Paragraph II(B)(1) constitutes non-taxable compensatory damages for alleged statutory violations of the Fair Credit Reporting Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1681 et seq., paid on account of personal injuries within the meaning of 26 U.S.C. § 104(a)(2). American Express agrees to characterize and report the Settlement Payment consistent with this treatment to the extent permitted by law. With respect to the balance waiver referenced in Paragraph II(B)(2), American Express shall provide Plaintiff with written notice, no fewer than thirty (30) days prior to issuance, of whether it intends to issue a Form 1099-C (Cancellation of Debt) for the $1,645.47 forgiven balance, including the amount to be reported and the identifiable event code relied upon. American Express cannot provide tax advice. Should Plaintiff have any questions or concerns regarding the tax treatment of the settlement, Plaintiff should consult with a tax advisor.
B. American Express.
In full and final settlement of Plaintiff's asserted and/or potential disputes, claims, and/or legal causes of action, inclusive of attorney's fees and costs, in the Action, American Express agrees to take the following action(s):
1. Payment: Within thirty (30) days of receipt by the American Express General Counsel's Organization, Center for Cardmember & Merchant Litigation of (1) this Agreement executed by Plaintiff, (2) an executed IRS Form W-9 (March 2024 version), which includes the tax identification number and/or social security number for Plaintiff, and (3) a completed copy of the Electronic Deposit Authorization Form attached hereto and incorporated herein as Exhibit "A", American Express shall issue an ACH transfer in the amount of One Thousand Dollars and Zero Cents ($1,000.00), made payable to Plaintiff ("Settlement Payment"), to their bank, as provided on Exhibit "A."
Plaintiff understands that American Express shall not process the settlement obligations until the completed and signed Agreement, completed and signed Forms W-9s, and Electronic Deposit Authorization Form are received by American Express. Plaintiff further understands and acknowledges that a delay might occur in processing the ACH transfer should there be any discrepancies and/or incorrect information provided on the Electronic Deposit Authorization Form.
2. Release of Liability: Within thirty (30) days of receipt by the American Express General Counsel's Organization, Center for Cardmember & Merchant Litigation of this Agreement executed by Plaintiff, American Express shall release Plaintiff from liability for the remaining outstanding balance of $1,645.47 on the Account.
3. Deletion of Reporting: Within thirty (30) days of receipt by the American Express General Counsel's Organization, Center for Cardmember & Merchant Litigation of this Agreement executed by Plaintiff, American Express shall submit a request to the following consumer reporting agencies, requesting that they delete any information relating to the Account from Plaintiff's consumer credit reports: Equifax Information Services, LLC, Experian Information Solutions, Inc., and Trans Union LLC (collectively, the "CRAs"). Within five (5) business days of submitting such deletion requests, American Express shall provide Plaintiff with written confirmation of the submission, including copies of the completed e-OSCAR (Online Solution for Complete and Accurate Reporting) or Automated Universal Dataform (AUD) submissions transmitted to each CRA.
In the unlikely event that negative information regarding the Account appears on Plaintiff's consumer credit reports with any of the CRAs more than sixty (60) days after American Express submits its request pursuant to this Paragraph, Plaintiff agrees to give American Express written notice of such report and thirty (30) days to request that the CRAs, or any one of them as the case may be, remove such information from Plaintiff's consumer credit report(s). The notice shall be sent to American Express General Counsel's Organization, Center for Cardmember and Merchant Litigation via email to CCML@aexp.com, and via mail to 1801 NW 66th Avenue, Suite 103, Plantation, Florida 33313, Mailcode FL05-03-58.
It is expressly agreed and stipulated by and between the Parties that American Express's sole obligation with respect to Plaintiff's credit history and credit history reporting associated with the Account is to communicate the above request to the CRAs, and that American Express has not and cannot make representations or guarantees as to the speed at which each of the CRAs will comply with such request from American Express, if at all. American Express shall use its reasonable best efforts to ensure that the CRAs process and complete the deletion request in a timely manner. If, after providing the confirmation described above, any CRA fails to delete the Account information within ninety (90) days of American Express's submission, American Express shall, upon written request from Plaintiff, re-submit the deletion request and provide Plaintiff with confirmation of such re-submission. Nothing in this Paragraph shall be construed to waive Plaintiff's rights under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1681 et seq., with respect to any conduct or omission by American Express occurring after the Effective Date.
Once American Express completes and submits the necessary forms to the CRAs to request deletion of the Account, American Express's obligations under this Agreement are fully satisfied and Plaintiff waives any and all claims, whether arising in contract or tort, common law or statute, and/or federal or state law (including, but not limited to, claims for any damages, attorneys' fees and/or costs), against American Express that may arise subsequent to the date of this Agreement. [The preceding paragraph is stricken in its entirety. Plaintiff's release under Article III is limited to claims arising prior to the Effective Date. Plaintiff retains all rights to enforce the terms of this Agreement and to bring claims based on conduct occurring after the Effective Date, including but not limited to claims under the Fair Credit Reporting Act for failure to comply with the deletion obligations set forth in this Paragraph.]
III. Release.
A. Release by Plaintiff.
Except as to such rights or claims as may be created by this Agreement, in consideration for the good and valuable consideration as set forth herein, receipt of which is hereby acknowledged, Plaintiff, on behalf of Plaintiff and Plaintiff's affiliates, attorneys, spouses, heirs and assigns, hereby specifically releases and forever discharges American Express National Bank, American Express Company, American Express Travel Related Services Company, Inc., and their directors, officers, agents, employees, representatives, servants, attorneys, predecessors, successors, assigns, subsidiaries, parent, vendors, collection affiliated entities (the "Released Parties"), from any and all claims, actions, causes of action, demands, liabilities, or obligations of any nature or kind, whether at present known or unknown, upon or by reason of any damage, agencies and loss or injury, which heretofore has been or which hereafter may be sustained by the undersigned, or for any other matters whatsoever from the beginning of the world to the Effective Date related to the subject matters of the Action and the Account (the "Release"). Plaintiff further agrees that Plaintiff will not file any claims, complaints, subpoenas, affidavits, arbitrations and/or proceedings with any court, arbitration forum, regulatory or administrative agency ("Proceedings") against the aforementioned Released Parties with respect to the matters released in this Agreement, and any such Proceedings filed prior to the execution of this Agreement shall promptly be dismissed or withdrawn, with prejudice. This Agreement is intended to resolve forever the entire disagreement between Plaintiff and American Express related to the Action and the Account.
B. Release by Defendant.
In consideration for the mutual covenants and obligations set forth herein, receipt of which is hereby acknowledged, American Express National Bank, on behalf of itself and its affiliates, parents, subsidiaries, predecessors, successors, assigns, directors, officers, agents, employees, representatives, servants, and attorneys (collectively, the "Defendant Releasing Parties"), hereby specifically releases and forever discharges Plaintiff, and Plaintiff's heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and assigns, from any and all claims, actions, causes of action, demands, liabilities, or obligations of any nature or kind, whether at present known or unknown, whether arising in contract or tort, common law or statute, and/or federal or state law, from the beginning of the world to the Effective Date, arising out of or related to the Account, the Action, or any dispute, transaction, or occurrence underlying the Action (the "Mutual Release"). American Express further agrees that it will not file any claims, complaints, arbitrations, and/or proceedings against Plaintiff with respect to the matters released in this Paragraph, and any such proceedings filed prior to the execution of this Agreement shall promptly be dismissed or withdrawn, with prejudice. This Mutual Release is intended to resolve forever the entire disagreement between the Parties related to the Action and the Account.
IV. General Provisions
A. Warranties By Plaintiff.
Plaintiff represents and warrants that Plaintiff has not assigned, transferred, or attempted to transfer, any claim or claims against American Express and that no other person or legal entity has any interest in any such claim or claims.
B. Authority.
Each Party to this Agreement represents and warrants that it has the full right, legal capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement and carry out the obligations noted herein and that the person executing this Agreement on its behalf has full authority and capacity to execute this Agreement.
C. No Admission of Liability.
This Agreement affects the settlement of claims/the Action that are denied and contested, and nothing contained in this Agreement will be construed as an admission by American Express, or its affiliated companies, either Party of any liability or wrongdoing. Plaintiff alleged damages as defined in 26 U.S.C. § 104(a)(2), but the parties have resolved the matter without determining liability.
D. Confidentiality.
Plaintiff expressly understands and agrees that the terms and provisions of this of the Settlement Payment, negotiations leading up to this settlement, and the existence of a settlement Agreement and its content, including but not limited to the fact of the Settlement Payment, amount between the Parties or the Agreement itself are strictly confidential in nature (hereinafter "Confidential Matters") and shall not be disclosed or published by Plaintiff either Party for any reason, except to Plaintiff's the respective Party's attorney or tax professional, or if Plaintiff the disclosing Party shall become legally compelled by operation of law. If Plaintiff a Party becomes legally compelled to disclose the Confidential Matters, Plaintiff that Party will promptly provide written notice to American Express General Counsel's Organization, Center for Cardmember and Merchant Litigation via email to CCML@aexp.com and mail to 1801 NW 66th Avenue, Suite 103, Plantation, Florida 33313, Mailcode FL05-03-58, the other Party via the notice addresses set forth in this Agreement, prior to disclosure and will cooperate with American Express's the other Party's reasonable efforts to obtain a protective order or other confidential treatment of the Confidential Matters or Agreement and its contents. If a protective order or other confidential treatment is not obtained or if American Express the other Party waives any portion of this provision, Plaintiff the disclosing Party agrees to furnish only so much of the Agreement or its contents as is legally required and, where appropriate, to exercise Plaintiff's its best efforts to obtain written assurances that confidential treatment will be accorded to the Confidential Matters and this Agreement and its contents.
E. Non-Disclosure.
Plaintiff Each Party (the "Receiving Party") agrees, for Plaintiff itself and Plaintiff's its affiliates, agents, attorneys, employees, directors, officers, representatives, assigns, executors, insurers, shareholders, and all of the its predecessors and successors, that Plaintiff the Receiving Party shall not directly or indirectly disclose, divulge, communicate, display, publish, or reveal the Confidential Matters defined herein through any medium, either orally or in writing, including, but not limited to, electronic mail, television or radio, computer networks or Internet bulletin boards, blogs, travel websites, social media, such as Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, Snapchat, or Twitter, or any other form of communication to any third party whatsoever including without limitation, any party to an administrative action, arbitration, mediation, or litigation against American Express the other Party or its affiliates except: (1) to Plaintiff's the Receiving Party's attorney or tax professional, or if Plaintiff the Receiving Party is legally compelled by operation of law; or (2) to enforce the terms or conditions of this Agreement and of the settlement reached between the Parties.
With respect to each of the limited non-disclosure exceptions set forth above in this Section IV(E), Plaintiff the Receiving Party understands and agrees that Plaintiff it shall inform the persons to whom Plaintiff it discloses information regarding the Confidential Matters or this Agreement of the confidential nature of this Agreement and shall use reasonable efforts to ensure that such persons shall agree to maintain the confidentiality of such Confidential Matters in accordance with the terms hereof. Plaintiff acknowledges and agrees that Plaintiff shall be liable for any breaches of confidentiality by any persons identified in the limited exceptions set forth above to whom information concerning the Confidential Matters or this Agreement is disclosed. The Receiving Party shall not be liable for breaches of confidentiality by such persons, provided the Receiving Party (a) informed such persons of the confidential nature of the Confidential Matters, (b) used reasonable efforts to obtain their agreement to maintain confidentiality, and (c) upon learning of any unauthorized disclosure, promptly notified the other Party and took reasonable steps to mitigate further disclosure.
F. Non-Disparagement.
Plaintiff agrees that it will not, in any manner, directly or indirectly, expressly or by innuendo, disparage, scorn or deprecate American Express, or its affiliated entities, regarding the matters that are the subject of the Action at any time, now or in the future, for any reason whatsoever. Each Party agrees that it will not, in any manner, directly or indirectly, expressly or by innuendo, disparage, scorn or deprecate the other Party, or its affiliated entities, regarding the matters that are the subject of the Action at any time, now or in the future, for any reason whatsoever. This mutual obligation applies equally to American Express and its agents, collection affiliates, and representatives, and to Plaintiff.
G. Attorney's Fees and Costs.
Each of the Parties will bear its own fees, costs and expenses incurred in connection with the Action and the disputes between the Parties which are the subject of, or related to, this Agreement, including, without limitations, the negotiation, drafting and consummation of this Agreement.
H. Entire Agreement.
This Agreement contains the entire agreement and understanding between the Parties concerning the subject matter. No provision of this Agreement may be waived unless in writing and signed by all Parties. This Agreement may not be altered, amended, or otherwise changed or modified, except in writing and signed by all Parties.
I. Choice of Law and Jurisdiction.
The Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Utah without regard to choice of law rules. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Georgia without regard to choice of law rules. The Parties agree that any action to enforce or interpret the terms of this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Gwinnett County, Georgia, and the Parties hereby consent to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.
J. Legal Advice.
Each Party has had the opportunity to consult with independent legal counsel with respect to the advisability of making this Agreement. Each Party has read and fully understands all of the provisions of this Agreement and is voluntarily entering into this Agreement.
K. Severability.
If any part of this Agreement is void or otherwise invalid, such invalid or void portion will be deemed to be separate and severable from the balance of this Agreement, and will be given full force and effect as though the void or invalid provisions had never been a part of the Agreement.
L. Construction.
Each term and provision of this Agreement shall be construed and interpreted so as to render it enforceable. This Agreement shall be deemed to have been drafted jointly by the Parties; accordingly, any rule pertaining to the construction of contracts to the effect that ambiguities are to be resolved against the drafting party shall not apply to the interpretation of this Agreement or of any modification of or amendment to this Agreement.
M. Execution in Counterparts.
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and all counterparts collectively will constitute one agreement binding on all Parties.
N. Electronic Signatures.
Faxed signatures, emailed electronic scans of original signatures, and DocuSign signatures shall be deemed originals. Each party agrees to sign this agreement by electronic signature and that this method of signature is as conclusive of our intention to be bound by this Agreement as if signed by each party's manuscript signature.
