What Is This?

The Bureau of Reclamation is buying up to 10 years of support for its RiverWare water-management modeling software — the winner provides software licenses, annual maintenance, software updates, technical support, software-engineering services, and training under a firm-fixed-price IDIQ. The Government has already determined, through an approved sole-source justification, that the University of Colorado (the software's developer) is the only responsible source able to perform this work (pp. 7, 28, 30-31).

Watch-Outs & Deal-Breakers

  • This is a sole-source to the University of Colorado: the Government has already determined it is the only responsible source and technical capability is NOT an evaluated factor. A competing offer must still confirm in Volume I that it is the owner and exclusive licensor of RiverWare and must pass the responsibility determination (FAR Part 9) or it cannot win (pp. 28, 30-32).
  • Proposals must be organized into the three named volumes — Volume I (Compliance Information, max 5 pages), Volume II (Price Proposal), Volume III (Administrative Information) — each as a separate searchable PDF with the exact naming convention. Failure to submit all required volumes may result in the proposal being determined unacceptable (pp. 27-28).
  • Order limitations apply: minimum order $2,500; maximum order $1,000,000 for a single item or a combination of items, including a series of orders from the same office within 30 days. An order exceeding the maximum must be returned in writing within 10 days or it must be honored (p. 23).
  • Price is the sole evaluation factor and the Government intends to award without discussions, so initial pricing must be best pricing; the offer acceptance period is 60 calendar days, and offer prices must remain firm for that entire period (pp. 28-32).
