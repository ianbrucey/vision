Scope of Work

The Department of the Interior, Bureau of Reclamation (Upper Colorado Region) is establishing a single-award Indefinite-Delivery/Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ) contract for IMT RiverWare Software Support under Solicitation 140R4026Q0099 (p. 1). The contractor will provide the proprietary RiverWare software licenses, annual software maintenance and enterprise support, software updates, technical support, software engineering services, training, and related services required under the Performance Work Statement (PWS), which is incorporated as Section J, Attachment 1 (pp. 7, 12, 25, 28). The contract consists of a base year (10/1/2026 – 9/30/2027) and nine (9) option periods running through 9/30/2036, priced as firm-fixed-price CLINs, with Other Direct Costs (ODCs) and travel priced at the individual task-order level (pp. 7–11, 20, 28). Award is intended on a sole-source basis to the University of Colorado, the owner and exclusive licensor of the proprietary RiverWare software; price is the sole evaluation factor for award (pp. 28, 31).

Contract Type

Indefinite-Delivery/Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ) contract with firm-fixed-price (FFP) pricing — the contract incorporates the FAR 52.216-22 Indefinite Quantity clause (pp. 23-24). The Contractor shall propose firm-fixed prices for all pre-priced Contract Line Item Numbers (CLINs) for the base period and each option period; prices established at contract award remain firm for the applicable contract period (Section H, p. 20). Proposed firm-fixed labor rates for Technical Support and Software Engineering Services CLINs establish the contract labor rates for future task orders (p. 32). Other Direct Cost (ODC) and Travel CLINs are established as FFP CLINs at the individual task-order level when required (p. 20). Order limitations apply: minimum order $2,500.00; maximum order $1,000,000.00 per single item or combination of items (52.216-19, p. 23).
