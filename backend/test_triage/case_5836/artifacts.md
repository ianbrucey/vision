## ARTIFACT 1: Scope of Work & Technical Requirements

### What is being procured
- **Single-award IDIQ contract** for **IMT RiverWare Software Support** for the Department of the Interior, Bureau of Reclamation, Upper Colorado Region.
- RiverWare is a **proprietary river & reservoir system modeling / water-management software** owned and exclusively licensed by the **University of Colorado** (CADSWES — Center for Advanced Decision Support for Water and Environmental Systems).
- Services procured under task orders: **software licenses, annual software maintenance, software updates/releases, software engineering services, technical support, training**, and related services.
- **Sole-source acquisition** — the Government has an approved sole-source justification determining the **University of Colorado is the only responsible source** capable of meeting the requirement.

### Contract structure
- **IDIQ** (Indefinite-Delivery/Indefinite-Quantity), **single award**, issued as a **Request for Quote (RFQ)** for commercial items.
- **Contract type:** Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) CLINs for Annual RiverWare Software Maintenance and Enterprise Support; FFP labor rates for Technical Support and Software Engineering Services (these rates set the contract rates for future task orders); **ODCs and Travel are NOT pre-priced** — priced FFP at the individual task-order level.
- **Period of Performance:** 10/01/2026 – 09/30/2036 (Base Year + **9 option periods**). Maximum contract duration incl. options: **126 months**. Option extension (52.217-9) max 126 months; short-term service extension (52.217-8) up to 6 months.
- **Ordering:** Task/delivery orders issued 10/1/2026 – 9/30/2036. **Min order $2,500; max order $1,000,000** per single or combined item; series of orders from same office within 30 days aggregated toward the max.
- **PSC:** DA10 (IT & Telecom — Business Application/App Development Software as a Service); one option period (VII) listed as D319 (Annual Software Maintenance Service Plans).

### Key technical requirements
- Offeror must be the **owner and exclusive licensor of the proprietary RiverWare software** and capable of delivering licenses, maintenance, software updates, engineering services, technical support, training, and related services (MANDATORY).
- **Technical capability is NOT an evaluated factor** — established via the sole-source justification. Volume I is compliance-only, not scored.
- **Firm-fixed pricing required for base + every option period**, inclusive of all direct labor, indirect costs, overhead, G&A, profit, materials, and projected escalation/inflation. Prices established at award remain firm for the applicable period.
- ODCs/Travel: proposed FFP at task-order level, evaluated for fairness/reasonableness per FAR 13.106-3 / 15.404-1(b).
- **Travel:** not allowed until authorized by the CO, a task order, or the PWS; priced as a separate FFP travel CLIN per task order using **Joint Travel Regulations (JTR)** as basis; no adjustment for actual costs.

### Location / place of performance
- Bureau of Reclamation, Upper Colorado Regional Office, **125 South State Street, Suite 8100, Salt Lake City, UT 84138** (FOB Destination). Software support/services are delivered to/for this office; remote service delivery anticipated.

### Performance metrics / SLAs / deliverables
- Governed by **Quality Assurance Surveillance Plan (QASP)** — Attachment 2 (referenced; full text not provided in these documents). Inspection/acceptance per Section E (blank in doc, per QASP/PWS).
- Full technical detail lives in **Attachment 1: Performance Work Statement (PWS)** and **Attachment 4: CADSWES Ordering Guide** (referenced; not included in provided package).

### Security & compliance requirements
- **No clearance level** (no SECRET/TS) stated in the solicitation.
- **No explicit NIST SP 800-171 / CMMC / FedRAMP** requirement stated in provided sections.
- Applicable clauses: **52.240-90** Security Prohibitions and Exclusions Reps & Certs and **52.241-91** Security Prohibitions and Exclusions — contractor must not violate federal security prohibitions/exclusions.
- **SAM registration + UEI** required and maintained (52.204-7 / 52.204-13). Contractor Government Business POC in SAM receives IPP enrollment instructions post-award.
- **Service Contract Reporting** required (52.204-15, IDIQ) — note for compliance.

### Mandatory (shall/must) vs Desirable (should/may)
- **Mandatory:** Own/exclusively license RiverWare; propose firm-fixed prices for all pre-priced CLINs (base + all options) with full escalation; maintain SAM/UEI; submit via IPP for invoicing; provide CPARS-designated reps within 30 days of award; acknowledge PWS and solicitation terms; hold offer firm 60 days; obtain CO authorization before incurring travel.

## ARTIFACT 3: Sourcing Script (Internal Sourcing Team)

### What type of work this is
Sole-source, 10-year IDIQ for proprietary RiverWare water-modeling software support (licenses, maintenance, engineering services, support, training) for the Bureau of Reclamation — the prime is effectively the software owner, the University of Colorado/CADSWES; there is no technical competition.

### What type of business to contact
- **Prime/source:** University of Colorado (CADSWES) — the sole, exclusive licensor of RiverWare. Also consider any authorized RiverWare support/implementation partners and water-resources engineering firms with RiverWare expertise that could serve as subcontractors or provide market-price benchmarking.
- **NAICS/expertise:** 541511 (Custom Computer Programming Services); look for firms with Bureau of Reclamation / water-resource systems modeling experience, RiverWare implementation, and DoD/DOI IT services contracting.

### 5–7 questions to ask for an actionable quote
1. Confirm you are the owner and exclusive licensor of RiverWare (or authorized reseller/support partner) and can commit to the full 10-year IDIQ term — what is your SAM UEI and registration status?
2. Provide a firm-fixed unit price for Annual RiverWare Software Maintenance and Enterprise Support for the base year and each of the 9 option periods — how is year-over-year escalation/inflation built in?
3. Provide firm-fixed labor rates (fully loaded) for each labor category under Technical Support and Software Engineering Services — which categories map to the PWS/CADSWES ordering guide?
4. What is the basis of estimate: labor mix, hours by category, indirect rates, G&A, profit, and escalation assumptions per period?
5. What ODCs and travel would you anticipate per task order (site visits, model runs, training), and can you price them FFP per JTR if awarded?
6. What is your current capacity/support model — SLA response times for support, release cadence for updates, and continuity of engineering staff for a 10-year term?
7. Can you hold these prices firm for 60 days and support task-order pricing within the $2.5K–$1M order limits without renegotiating contract-level rates?

### Key pricing considerations
- Price is the **sole evaluation factor** — fairness/reasonableness benchmarked against IGCE, historical pricing, and the offeror's basis of estimate (FAR 13.106-3 / 15.404-1(b)).
- All **pre-priced CLINs are FFP for the entire period** — escalation/inflation risk sits with the contractor; do not rely on future repricing. Only the 52.217-8 short-term extension allows labor-rate adjustment.
- ODCs/Travel are **unpriced at contract level** — priced FFP per task order; don't bake them into base pricing.
- **Unbalanced option prices** could make the offer unacceptable — spread escalation consistently across option periods.
- Watch total contract ceiling: min order $2.5K, max $1M per order; duration max 126 months.

### Gotchas / deal-breakers
- **Offer deadline discrepancy (08/20 vs 08/25)** — confirm with the CO before scheduling anything.
- Do **NOT** spend effort on a technical volume — Volume I is compliance-only (5-page cap) and not scored.
- Must be the owner/exclusive licensor of RiverWare — any other offeror is non-compliant and will be rejected.
- Must commit to **firm pricing for 10 years** — a quote with escalation "to be negotiated later" is non-responsive.
- Mandatory **IPP** invoicing + hardcopy cc to both CO and COR — non-compliance delays payment.
- **CPARS** rep designation within 30 days of award; SAM/UEI must be active.
- Only the CO can authorize changes or travel — COR technical direction has strict limits; notify CO in writing within 5 working days if direction exceeds scope.

### Timeline urgency
- **Very tight.** Issued 08/10/2026; offers due **08/20 or 08/25/2026** (10–15 days), award without discussions expected, and the base period starts **10/01/2026**. Lock pricing, SAM/UEI verification, and the compliance statement this week — there is no room for last-minute corrections.
