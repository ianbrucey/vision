  • Deadline discrepancy: the full Combined Synopsis/Solicitation shows offer due 08/20/2026 17:00 ES (p. 5), while the SF-1449 form shows 08/25/2026 17:00 ED (p. 1) — confirm the correct date before submitting.

  • Electronic invoicing is mandatory: payment requests go through the Treasury Invoice Processing Platform (https://www.ipp.gov), and a hard copy of each invoice must also be emailed to the CO and COR or payment may be delayed or rejected (p. 16).
