What Is This?

The National Weather Service needs a contractor to restore the aging brick and mortar exterior of its Pendleton, Oregon Weather Forecast Office and the adjacent generator building — pressure-washing, repairing, patching, resealing, and replacing expansion joints on roughly 5,363 square feet of brick to bring back the buildings' aesthetic and functional integrity (pp. 3-5).
