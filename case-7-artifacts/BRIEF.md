THE ASK:
The Department of War seeks industry input on building a cloud-native, multi-tenant enterprise document management platform on JWCC (IL5) to ingest 1.5 billion digitized microfiche records, subsume the legacy CEDMS system (CDA and MSS subsystems), and serve as the Department's standardized, API-first document fabric with embedded AI/ML analytics.

  Facility Clearance: Not stated (RFI).
  Personnel Clearance: Not stated (RFI). IL5 implies U.S. persons requirement.
  Other: IL5 (DoD Impact Level 5) mandatory. JWCC infrastructure mandatory. Zero-Trust / ICAM / RBAC required. MOSA statutory. RFI response is UNCLASSIFIED.

PLACE: Not stated in RFI. Work is cloud-native on JWCC infrastructure; no on-site military base required.

RESPONSE:
  Due: June 15, 2026, 1:00 PM EST (5 calendar days)
  Page Limit: 25 pages (hard maximum)
  Submit To: Gerald.l.whitsett.civ@mail.mil
  Format: PDF or Word (white paper, UNCLASSIFIED)

SCOPE SUMMARY:
- Build cloud-native DMS on JWCC at IL5, replacing legacy CEDMS-CDA (financial document archival) and CEDMS-MSS (SharePoint/Altair Monarch report processing).
- Ingest and index ~1.5B records from 5.88M physical microfiche sheets digitized to FADGI 3-Star (dual-file TIFF/PDF with metadata manifests).
- Deliver Intelligent Document Processing (OCR/NLP/AI/ML) for autonomous classification, metadata extraction, and cross-document trend analytics.
- Multi-tenant architecture with data isolation, API-first integration, Zero-Trust ICAM at the document level, and MOSA compliance.
- Provide migration roadmap for CEDMS subsumption; ROM cost estimate; past performance references (3-5 years); CAGE/UEI; small business status.
