8. Strategic Recommendations

1. Prioritize Small Business set-asides over unrestricted-under-SAT.
There are 3,548 SB set-aside opportunities vs. just 16 unrestricted under $500K. The SAT subcontracting strategy should be a fallback, not the primary play. Focus on SB set-asides at any dollar value — the government is actively carving out large contracts for small business.
2. Target Interior's Bureau of Reclamation and Fish & Wildlife Service.
These two offices alone have 1,111 Under-SAT opportunities combined (59% of all Under-SAT). They're the most accessible entry points for small vendors. Their top NAICS codes are construction, environmental services, and facility maintenance.
3. The TBD pipeline is the opportunity.
3,541 opportunities (48%) haven't determined their set-aside strategy yet. Early engagement with these contracting offices — before the solicitation drops — could influence the set-aside determination. 1,029 of these are under SAT.
4. IT services is the largest accessible market.
NAICS 541519 (Other Computer Related Services) has 866 opportunities, 271 under SAT. This is the single largest accessible category for small IT vendors. Combined with 541512 (Computer Systems Design), there are over 1,000 IT opportunities.
5. Move fast on FY 2026.
73% of opportunities are FY 2026 with most response dates in the next 3–6 months. The pipeline thins dramatically for FY 2027+. New forecasts are likely still being added by agencies — refresh the data monthly to capture them.
