9. Strategic Recommendations

1. The FY-end surge is happening NOW.
July posted 1,310 notices in a single week — 3× the normal weekly pace. With 2,391 deadlines in the next 30 days, agencies are in full Q4 spending mode. This is the time to be aggressive on outreach and proposal submission.
2. Construction trades are the SB sweet spot.
Highway construction (78% SB), plumbing/HVAC (53%), electrical (45%), building repair (76%). These have the highest small business participation rates in SAM.gov. If your vendor database has construction contractors, match them to these notices aggressively.
3. DoD is 71% of the market — but hard to penetrate directly.
DLA, Navy, Air Force, and Army dominate volume but have low SB set-aside rates (12–30%). The play here is subcontracting — identify the primes winning these unrestricted contracts and position as a subcontractor. DLA Land and Maritime (54 SB) and NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic (29 SB) are the exceptions.
4. VA is the SDVOSB gateway.
The VA has 85 SDVOSB set-asides — 72% of all SDVOSB notices nationwide. If your vendor database has SDVOSB-certified companies, the VA should be their primary target. The Technology Acquisition Center (36C10B) alone handles 622 VA notices.
5. Unrestricted combined synopses are the subcontracting play.
378 Combined Synopsis/Solicitations are unrestricted (no set-aside). These are formal RFPs where the prime will need subcontractors. Cross-reference these with the forecast data to identify which primes are likely bidders.
