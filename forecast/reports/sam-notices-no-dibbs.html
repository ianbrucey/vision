7. Strategic Recommendations

1. 72% are due within 30 days — this is the Q4 surge.
612 of 855 notices have deadlines in the next month. Agencies are obligating FY26 funds before the September 30 deadline. The next 4-6 weeks are the busiest procurement window of the year. Prioritize outreach and proposal turnaround speed.
2. Forest Service is the easiest win for SB.
43 of 44 notices (98%) are SB set-aside. Mostly construction, maintenance, and land management. Low competition compared to DoD. If you have any construction or forestry vendors, match them here immediately — 38 of the 44 are due within 30 days.
3. NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic and NSWC Indian Head are Navy SB hubs.
37 opportunities combined at 95% SB rate. These are facilities/support contracts, not weapons systems. NAVFAC does base construction/maintenance; NSWC Indian Head does energetics/ordnance support. Both are accessible to small businesses.
4. 313 SB set-asides are direct targets. 481 unrestricted are subcontracting plays.
Of the 481 unrestricted notices, 246 are formal solicitations (Combined Synopsis/Solicitation). The prime contractors winning these will need subs. Cross-reference with the forecast data to identify the likely primes. The other 235 unrestricted are Sources Sought — market research where you can influence the eventual set-aside determination.
5. Roofing, plumbing/HVAC, and electrical have the best SB rates.
Roofing: 13/14 SB. Plumbing/HVAC: 17/24 SB. Electrical: 14/27 SB. These construction trades consistently have the highest small business participation. If your vendor database has these trades, they should be your first-tier matching priority.

8. Methodology — SQL Queries Used

All queries run against the sam_notices table in PostgreSQL. The table is populated from the SAM.gov databank CSV via PostgreSQL COPY, deduplicated on notice_id.

Filter: Exclude DLA, keep only Solicitations + Sources Sought

-- Core filter: active, solicitation-types only, no DIBBS
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM sam_notices
WHERE status = 'active'
  AND contract_opportunity_type IN ('Combined Synopsis/Solicitation', 'Sources Sought')
  AND sub_tier_name != 'DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY';

-- Count by set-aside
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM sam_notices
WHERE status = 'active'
  AND contract_opportunity_type IN ('Combined Synopsis/Solicitation', 'Sources Sought')
  AND sub_tier_name != 'DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY'
  AND current_set_aside_code = 'SBA';  -- Small Business

SELECT COUNT(*) FROM sam_notices
WHERE status = 'active'
  AND contract_opportunity_type IN ('Combined Synopsis/Solicitation', 'Sources Sought')
  AND sub_tier_name != 'DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY'
  AND (current_set_aside_code IS NULL
       OR current_set_aside_code = ''
       OR current_set_aside_code = 'NONE');  -- Unrestricted

-- Due within 30 days
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM sam_notices
WHERE status = 'active'
  AND contract_opportunity_type IN ('Combined Synopsis/Solicitation', 'Sources Sought')
  AND sub_tier_name != 'DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY'
  AND current_response_date IS NOT NULL
  AND current_response_date >= NOW()
  AND current_response_date <= NOW() + INTERVAL '30 days';

Agency Breakdown

SELECT sub_tier_name, COUNT(*) as total,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE current_set_aside_code = 'SBA') as sba,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE current_set_aside_code = 'SDVOSBC') as sdvosb,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE contract_opportunity_type = 'Combined Synopsis/Solicitation') as sols,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE contract_opportunity_type = 'Sources Sought') as ss,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE current_response_date >= NOW()
                        AND current_response_date <= NOW() + INTERVAL '30 days') as due_30d
FROM sam_notices
WHERE status = 'active'
  AND contract_opportunity_type IN ('Combined Synopsis/Solicitation', 'Sources Sought')
  AND sub_tier_name != 'DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY'
GROUP BY sub_tier_name
ORDER BY total DESC;

Set-Aside Categorization

SELECT CASE
    WHEN current_set_aside_code = 'SBA' THEN 'Small Business'
    WHEN current_set_aside_code = 'SDVOSBC' THEN 'SDVOSB'
    WHEN current_set_aside_code = 'WOSB' THEN 'Women-Owned'
    WHEN current_set_aside_code = 'HZC' THEN 'HUBZone'
    WHEN current_set_aside_code = '8A' THEN '8(a)'
    WHEN current_set_aside_code IS NULL OR current_set_aside_code = ''
         OR current_set_aside_code = 'NONE' THEN 'Unrestricted/None'
    ELSE current_set_aside_code END as category,
    COUNT(*) as total,
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE contract_opportunity_type = 'Combined Synopsis/Solicitation') as sols,
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE contract_opportunity_type = 'Sources Sought') as ss
FROM sam_notices
WHERE status = 'active'
  AND contract_opportunity_type IN ('Combined Synopsis/Solicitation', 'Sources Sought')
  AND sub_tier_name != 'DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY'
GROUP BY category
ORDER BY total DESC;
