You are dialing into the exact sweet spot of government contracting: **high-velocity, low-friction, drop-ship or clear-scope opportunities.**

Filtering out complex IT service delivery and heavy construction management is a smart operational decision. Managing crews, milestones, and technical labor adds massive overhead. By focusing on **pure reselling** and **simple, "snipeable" fixed-scope subcontracts**, you can run this entire operation lean using your automation engine and a small support team.

---

## Sector 1: IT Hardware & Software Reselling (Drop-Ship / Direct Renewal)

* **The Scope:** Pure software renewals, off-the-shelf software licenses, and standard brand-name hardware (Cisco, Fortinet, Dell, HP). No labor, no custom code, no ongoing IT service delivery.
* **Target NAICS:**
* `541519` (Other Computer Related Services / Software Reselling)
* `513210` / `511210` (Software Publishers)
* `334111` / `334220` (Computer Terminal & Network Equipment)
* **Target Opportunities:**
* **Department of Veterans Affairs (TAC NJ):** Frequent software license renewals and subscriptions (e.g., Adobe, VMware, specialized medical IT software).
* **Department of the Interior:** Network equipment replacements and Cloud/Azure commitments (e.g., Cisco switches, Fortinet firewalls).
* **Execution Strategy:**
* Run automated scrapers on active SAM notices looking for exact manufacturer part numbers.
* Feed part numbers directly to distributors (Carahsoft, Ingram Micro, TD SYNNEX) to retrieve instant wholesale quotes.

---

## Sector 2: "Snipeable" Trade & Utility Services (Subcontractor Integration)

* **The Scope:** Routine, essential, non-complex physical work under $350K (the Simplified Acquisition Threshold). These are clear-cut jobs that a local contractor can price out in 15 minutes without extensive engineering drawings or multi-month site surveys.
* **Target NAICS:**
* `562111` (Solid Waste Collection & Waste Disposal)
* `561720` (Janitorial Services) & `561730` (Landscaping/Mowing)
* `238210` (Electrical Wiring & Panel Upgrades)
* `238220` (HVAC Unit Replacements / Filter Maintenance)
* `561622` (Locksmith & Security Hardware Installation)
* **Target Opportunities:**
* **U.S. Bureau of Reclamation & Fish and Wildlife Service:** These two offices alone hold over 1,100 Under-SAT opportunities (59% of all Under-SAT forecasts)—mostly routine facility repairs, fencing, dumpster rentals, and groundskeeping.
* **Execution Strategy:**
* Position your business to local tradesmen as their **Federal Sales & Compliance Partner**.
* Your pitch to local tradesmen: *"We hold the prime contract, manage SAM compliance, deal with federal billing, and absorb the administrative burden—you simply perform the physical trade work as our subcontractor."*

---

## Sector 3: Specialty Goods & Freight-Ready US Manufacturers (Drop-Ship)

* **The Scope:** Commercial Off-The-Shelf (COTS) equipment manufactured in the U.S. where the factory/distributor handles crate packaging and direct freight shipping to an agency depot or park station.
* **Target NAICS:**
* `336999` / `336991` (Specialized Motor Vehicles: Snowmobiles, ATVs, Utility Carts for Forestry/Park Services)
* `335312` (Generators and Emergency Power Systems)
* `334516` / `339113` (Analytical Laboratory Equipment & Specialized Medical/Testing Supplies)
* `332311` (Prefabricated Metal Buildings / Storage Containers)
* **Execution Strategy:**
* Focus on solicitations with strict **Buy American Act (BAA)** or **Trade Agreements Act (TAA)** clauses.
* Partner with U.S. manufacturers who are already set up to ship via LTL (Less-Than-Truckload) freight. You secure the federal order; they ship it directly to the military base or national park.

## Candid Advice: How to Start Small

> **Don't target 100 solicitations on day one.** Pick **5 active, simple solicitations** across these categories first. Use your pipeline to build the draft emails, manually review each one to ensure the messaging is tight, and get your first 2–3 quotes back from vendors or tradesmen. Once the pipeline loop works end-to-end for 5 items, scale it to 50.
