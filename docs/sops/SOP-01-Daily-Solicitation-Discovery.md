# SOP-01: Daily Solicitation Discovery & Claiming Protocol

> **Role:** Sourcing Specialist (Research Agent)  
> **Target:** 5–10 Qualified Solicitations Claimed per Agent / Day  
> **Frequency:** Daily (Monday – Friday)  
> **Goal:** Identify prime contract opportunities that fit our subcontractor delivery network, lock them to prevent team overlap, and advance them to sourcing.

---

## 2. Portal Search Procedure

### Primary Source: SAM.gov Contract Opportunities

1. Navigate to [SAM.gov](https://sam.gov) → **Contract Opportunities**.
2. Apply mandatory filters:
   - **Notice Type:** `Solicitation`, `Combined Synopsis/Solicitation`, or `Sources Sought` (if gathering early intelligence). *Do not select Pre-solicitation or Justification unless instructed.*
   - **Set-Aside:** Select `Total Small Business Set-Aside (SBA)`, `Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned (SDVOSB)`, `Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB)`, or `HUBZone Set-Aside`.
   - **NAICS Code:** Enter your assigned NAICS codes (from Section 1).
   - **Response Due Date:** Filter for deadlines **at least 10 to 21 business days out**. *Discard opportunities closing in under 7 days unless the SOW is trivial.*
   - **Active Only:** Check `Active`.

### Secondary Sources
- **DLA DIBBS:** For standard commercial off-the-shelf and supply/hardware procurements.
- **State / Local Portals (e.g., Georgia DOAS, California Cal eProcure):** When federal volume in your sector is slow.

## 3. The 3-Minute Qualification Triage

Before claiming any bid, spend no more than 3 minutes reviewing the documentation against the **Triage Checklist**:

1. **Clear Scope of Work:** Is there a defined Statement of Work / Performance Work Statement (PWS) that a commercial sub can quote? (Pass = Yes).
2. **Subcontractable Trades:** Can 100% of the field work be performed by a licensed trade partner? (Pass = Yes).
3. **Realistic Timeline:** Can we reasonably source 2–3 quotes before the proposal deadline? (Pass = Yes).
4. **Dealbreakers (Automatic Rejections):**
   - ❌ Requires facility security clearance (Top Secret / SCI) that cannot be sponsored.
   - ❌ Sole source or proprietary manufacturer-locked specifications.
   - ❌ Bids requiring mandatory pre-bid site visits within 48 hours in remote areas without an identified local sub.

---

## 4. Claiming & Collision Prevention

Once a solicitation passes the 3-minute triage:

1. **Check System Existing Entries:** Open the internal portal → Solicitations Search. Search by **Solicitation Number / Notice ID** (e.g., `W91248-26-R-0012`).
   - If the solicitation is **already claimed**, STOP immediately. Move to the next opportunity.
2. **Claim Solicitation:**
   - Click **Claim Solicitation**.
   - Select your Sector and confirm you are the assigned lead.
   - The status is immediately updated to `Claimed - Extraction Pending`.
3. **Upload Documentation:**
   - Upload the primary RFP document, SOW/PWS, and any wage determinations (Davis-Bacon / Service Contract Act) to the opportunity record.
