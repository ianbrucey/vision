# SOP-05: US Team Phone Escalation, Quote Intake & Submission Handoff

> **Role:** US Operations Team (Founder / Amani / Bree)  
> **Input:** Escalated Vendor Records & Received Quotes  
> **Output:** Verified Subcontractor Quotes, Prime Pricing Model, Bid Submission to CO  
> **Direct Line:** (470) 785-3007

---

## 1. Division of Labor & Escalation Trigger

To maintain maximum professionalism and trust with American trade contractors:
* **Offshore Sourcing Agents (Pakistan):** Handle search, structured extraction, initial email outreach, and thread logging. **Do not place cold phone calls.**
* **US Coordination Team:** Steps in whenever a phone call, live technical consultation, bonding discussion, or formal quote closing is needed.

### When to Trigger a Phone Call
1. Vendor replies to email saying *"Give me a call to discuss"* or has questions about government payment terms.
2. Vendor requested the scope packet but has not submitted numbers within 48 hours of the quote deadline.
3. High-dollar or complex solicitation ($150K+) where an active subcontractor relationship must be secured immediately.

## 2. Phone Call Script & Conversation Framework

When calling a prospective subcontractor from `(470) 785-3007`:

```
"Hi [Contact Name], this is [Your Name] with Gov Services Connect / Justice Quest. 

I’m following up on the email exchange we had with our sourcing team regarding the [Scope of Work, e.g., HVAC replacement] at [Facility / Base Name]. 

We’re finalizing our prime contractor submission for this project. I saw you had a couple questions on the scope / wanted to touch base to make sure you have everything you need to send over a number."
```

### Key Reassurance Points During the Call
* **We are the Prime:** "We carry the contract, the administrative compliance, the bonding (if required), and the invoicing through the government portal. You only have to worry about delivering the trade work."
* **Prompt Payment:** "We pay on approved progress milestones as outlined in our teaming agreement."
* **No Red Tape on their End:** "You don't need to write proposals or register in SAM.gov to quote us; we keep your COI and license on file."

## 3. Subcontractor Quote Intake Procedure

When a subcontractor sends their formal pricing/proposal:

1. **Open Solicitation Record** in the internal system.
2. Navigate to the **Quotes** tab.
3. Click **Add Subcontractor Quote**:
   - **Subcontractor Name:** Select vendor from the matched list.
   - **Total Quoted Price:** Enter exact amount (e.g., `$84,500.00`).
   - **Breakdown / Notes:** Note what is included (materials, labor, equipment rental, tax). Note any exclusions.
   - **Point of Contact:** Name, direct phone, and email of the estimating lead.
   - **Attach File:** Upload the vendor's original PDF / estimate sheet.
4. **Compare Multiple Quotes:** If 2 or more quotes are received for the same scope, evaluate:
   - Lowest price vs. technical responsiveness.
   - Verified active state license and insurance.
   - Geographic proximity to the site.

---

## 4. Final Prime Pricing & Submission Handoff

Once the preferred subcontractor quote is locked in:

1. **Calculate Prime Margin:**
   $$\text{Prime Bid Price} = \text{Subcontractor Quote} + \text{Prime Management / G&A Markup} + \text{Bonding (if applicable)}$$
   *Standard target margin is 12% to 25% depending on contract size and competition.*
2. **Assemble Bid Package:**
   - Completed SF-1449 / solicitation pricing schedule.
   - Technical approach / capability statement citing the subcontractor’s qualified team.
   - Subcontractor past performance references and licensing attachments.
   - Proof of Insurance / Teaming letter (if requested by CO).
3. **Founder / Designee Final Review:**
   - Verify SAM.gov registration (`21GM9` / `MU8FAL4JBL91`) is current.
   - Ensure all solicitation amendments (SF-30s) are acknowledged and signed.
4. **Submit to Contracting Officer:**
   - Email submission or submit via portal (PIEE, SAM.gov, DIBBS) prior to the official deadline.
   - Log submission timestamp and confirmation receipt in the system.
5. **Update Pipeline Status:**
   - Change solicitation status to `Submitted - Awaiting Award Decision`.
