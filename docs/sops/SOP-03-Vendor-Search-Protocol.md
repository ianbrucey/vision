# SOP-03: Multi-Channel Vendor Search & Discovery Protocol

> **Role:** Sourcing Specialist (Research Agent)  
> **Input:** Approved Sourcing Packet (from SOP-02)  
> **Target:** Identify 5–8 Qualified Vendor Contacts per Solicitation  
> **Rule:** Never rely solely on generic Google searches. Follow the tiered search matrix below to prevent paralysis.

---

## 1. The 4-Tier Search Hierarchy

When looking for subcontractors capable of fulfilling a government project, work through these tiers in order:

```
Tier 1: SBA Dynamic Small Business Search (DSBS)
   ↓ (If insufficient candidates)
Tier 2: USASpending.gov & Subcontracting Leads
   ↓ (If specialized licensing / GSA required)
Tier 3: GSA eLibrary & Schedule Holders
   ↓ (If local trade / specialized geography)
Tier 4: State Licensing Boards & Verified Commercial Registries
```

---

## 2. Tier 1: SBA Dynamic Small Business Search (DSBS)
*The most authoritative directory of registered, active small businesses in the USA.*

1. **Access:** Navigate to [SBA DSBS](https://dsbs.sba.gov/).
2. **Search Parameters:**
   - **Location:** Select the State where the project is located (or within 50–100 miles of the city/military base).
   - **NAICS Code:** Enter the 6-digit NAICS code from the Sourcing Packet.
   - **Keywords:** Enter key equipment or service terms (e.g., `"chiller"`, `"generator"`, `"asbestos"`, `"fiber optic"`).
   - **Status:** Active in SAM.gov.
3. **Candidate Evaluation:**
   - Look for companies with an active website, verified phone, and direct email address (avoid generic `sales@` or info boxes when personal contacts like `mike@company.com` are listed).
   - Check the **Capabilities Narrative** and **Past Performance** block in DSBS.
4. **Data to Capture:** Company Name, Owner/Contact Name, Email Address, Phone Number, UEI/CAGE Code, City/State.

---

## 3. Tier 2: USASpending.gov Subcontracting Leads
*Identify vendors that have already worked as subcontractors or prime contractors on similar contracts.*

1. **Access:** Internal Portal → Subcontracting Leads (`/subcontracting-leads`) or directly on [USASpending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov).
2. **Filter By:**
   - Awarding Agency (e.g., Dept of the Army, VA, GSA).
   - Primary NAICS code.
   - Recipient State.
3. **Why this works:** These vendors already understand government compliance, certified payroll, and federal base requirements.

---

## 4. Tier 3: GSA eLibrary
*For commercial products, IT services, and established facilities suppliers.*

1. **Access:** Navigate to [GSA eLibrary](https://www.gsaelibrary.gsa.gov/).
2. **Search by Schedule / SIN Category:**
   - Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) → Search by Category (e.g., Facilities Maintenance, IT Software, Industrial Supplies).
3. **Locate Vendors:** Click into the contract holders list for your geographical area and extract the Government Contract POC details.

---

## 5. Tier 4: State Licensing Boards & Commercial Trade Associations
*When local proximity is mandatory (e.g., emergency plumbing, state-licensed electrical, roofing).*

1. **State Licensing Verification:**
   - Search the state licensing portal (e.g., *Georgia Secretary of State Licensing Division*, *Texas TDLR*).
   - Search for active licensed master contractors in the project county.
2. **Trade Association Directories:**
   - NECA (Electrical Contractors Association)
   - PHCC (Plumbing-Heating-Cooling Contractors)
   - ABC (Associated Builders and Contractors) chapter directories.

---

## 6. Vendor Verification & Logging in the System

Before sending an outreach email:
1. Ensure the company is **in business** (check website, Google reviews/presence).
2. Ensure they are within a reasonable driving radius of the job site (for site-based services).
3. Log the vendor profile into the system under the solicitation record:
   - `Business Name` (Required)
   - `Contact Person Name` (Required)
   - `Direct Email` (Required)
   - `Phone Number` (Required)
   - `Website` (Optional but recommended)
   - `CAGE / UEI` (If discovered on DSBS/SAM)
