# SOP-02: Solicitation Deconstruction & Sourcing Packet Generation

> **Role:** Sourcing Specialist / Extraction Agent  
> **Input:** Claimed Solicitation Package (RFP, SOW/PWS, Attachments)  
> **Output:** Standardized 1-Page Sourcing Packet & Vendor Vetting Form  
> **Timeline:** Complete within 15–20 minutes of claiming a solicitation.

---

## 1. Purpose & Principle

Subcontractors and commercial trade vendors will **not read** a 100-page federal solicitation, FAR clauses, or government jargon. 

Our job is to act as the translation layer: extract the exact technical deliverables, certifications, and timeline, and compress them into a **clean 1-page Sourcing Packet** before any vendor outreach begins.

---

## 2. Information Extraction Checklist

Open the solicitation documents and extract the following 6 data blocks into the system:

### 1. Plain-English Statement of Work (SOW)
* Strip out legal and bureaucratic language.
* Describe in 3–5 bullet points:
  - What exact service or product is being provided?
  - What is the quantity / square footage / equipment count?
  - What are the working hours (normal business hours, nights/weekends, escort required)?

### 2. Location & Geographic Constraints
* Exact facility name, address, city, state, zip code (e.g., *Robins AFB, Warner Robins, GA 31098*).
* Base access requirements (e.g., Real ID, background check, pass & tag office).

### 3. Timeline & Period of Performance (PoP)
* Start date and completion duration (e.g., *60 calendar days from Notice to Proceed* or *1 Base Year + 4 Option Years*).
* Mandatory site visit date/time (if applicable).
* **Vendor Quote Deadline:** Set this **at least 3 to 5 business days BEFORE the government proposal deadline** so our team has time to assemble pricing.

### 4. Mandatory Qualifications & Licensing
Identify any non-negotiable thresholds the sub must meet:
* Required licenses (e.g., State Master HVAC, Electrical Contractor Class A).
* Required certifications (e.g., EPA 608 Universal, OSHA 30, BICSI, CompTIA Security+).
* Bonding capacity (if construction over $150K requires Bid/Performance/Payment bonds).
* Insurance minimums (General Liability $1M/$2M, Worker's Comp, Commercial Auto).

### 5. Prevailing Wage Determination
* Is this covered by **Davis-Bacon Act (DBA)** (construction) or **Service Contract Act (SCA)** (services)?
* Note the wage determination number and state/county so the sub knows prevailing wage rates apply.

### 6. The 3–5 Qualifying Questions
Draft 3 to 5 clear questions that any potential subcontractor must answer:
1. *Are you licensed and insured to perform work in [State/County]?*
2. *Can you meet the timeline of completing work within [Duration] starting [Target Date]?*
3. *Do your technicians have the ability to pass a standard federal background check for base access?*
4. *(Technical specific)* e.g., *Do you have experience replacing 50-ton Trane chillers or equivalent commercial units?*
5. *Can you provide a firm-fixed-price quote breakdown by [Quote Deadline]?*

---

## 3. Creating the System Sourcing Brief

In the internal system under the claimed solicitation record:
1. Navigate to the **Sourcing Brief** tab.
2. Paste the extracted plain-English SOW.
3. Check the required certification and insurance tags.
4. Input the **Target Quote Due Date**.
5. Save the record. The system will now automatically format this into the email outreach template.
