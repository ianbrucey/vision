## Responses to Specific Questions

**Q1: How would your solution address the migration of screening system datasets to the cloud while ensuring secure transfer and data integrity?**
A: Justice Quest LLC would leverage our proven zero-downtime migration methodology (PP-006) to move datasets to a secure cloud environment. We would implement AWS-based infrastructure with VPC isolation, multi-AZ redundancy, and encrypted data transfer protocols. Data integrity would be ensured through automated validation scripts (similar to PP-005’s Snowflake/dbt audits) and checksum verification at every stage of migration.

**Q2: How would your platform enable secure access to data for algorithm developers while maintaining compliance with SSI standards?**
A: We would design a role-based access control (RBAC) system with granular permissions, audit logging, and encrypted data-at-rest. Our experience with Elasticsearch-based indexing (PP-006) and multi-agent architectures (PP-001) demonstrates our ability to provide efficient, controlled access to sensitive data. For SSI compliance, we would team with a prime contractor holding the necessary clearances and frameworks.

**Q3: What is your approach to tracking and auditing data access for compliance and operational oversight?**
A: We would implement CloudWatch-style monitoring (PP-006) combined with custom audit trails for user access, data modifications, and usage metrics. Our past performance includes maintaining 24×7 cloud operations with automated alerts and logging, ensuring full traceability of all system interactions.
