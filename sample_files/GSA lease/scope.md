## Scope of Work

### What Is Being Procured

The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) is issuing this Request for Lease Proposals (RLP) to lease **office space** in the Port St. Lucie / Fort Pierce, Florida area. The Government seeks a **fully serviced lease** of **4,757 to 4,995 ANSI/BOMA Occupant Area (ABOA) square feet of contiguous space**
 within a delineated Area of Consideration. The RLP results in a direct
lease agreement between GSA and the successful Offeror (Lessor); this is
 not a construction or services contract. Offerors provide an existing
or to-be-built building and are compensated through rent.

### Place of Performance

**Port St. Lucie / Fort Pierce, Florida.** The space must be located within the following bounded Area of Consideration:

* **North:** From the intersection of Okeechobee Road
  and Delaware Avenue, proceed east on Delaware Avenue to its intersection
  with South U.S. Highway 1.
* **East & South:** Proceed south on South U.S.
  Highway 1 to W. White Clay Road; west on W. White Clay Road converging
  into W. Midway Road; west on W. Midway Road to Florida's Turnpike; south
  on Florida's Turnpike to Crosstown Parkway; west on Crosstown Parkway
  to Commerce Centre Drive; north on Commerce Centre Drive.
* **West:** North on Commerce Centre Drive to Champions
  Way; north on Champions Way to Reserve Blvd; west on Reserve Blvd to
  Commerce Centre Drive; north on Commerce Centre Drive to Glades Cut Off
  Road; northeast on Glades Cut Off Road to I-95; north on I-95 to SR 70;
  northeast on SR 70 to Okeechobee Road; northeast on Okeechobee Road to
  Delaware Avenue.

Buildings with property boundaries on the boundary streets are deemed within the Area of Consideration.

### Contract / Lease Type

This is a  **Request for Lease Proposals (RLP)** , not a standard federal contract. The award will be a **fully serviced lease**
 with rent paid per Rentable Square Foot (RSF), limited by the offered
rate and the maximum ABOA SF solicited. Award is based on **lowest price, technically acceptable**
 using a present value price evaluation of the total annual price per
ABOA SF over the full lease term (including options). This is not an
FFP, IDIQ, or T&M contract; it is a real property lease transaction.

### Lease Term

* **Total Term:** 15 years
* **Firm Term:** 10 years
* **Termination Rights:** Government may terminate, in
  whole or in part, at any time after the Firm Term with not less than 90
  days' prior written notice.
* **Occupancy:** Lease term commences upon completion and acceptance of the leased space (including Tenant Improvements).

### Key Requirements

### Mandatory (Shall / Must / Will)

1. **Space Quantity:** Offer must provide a minimum of
   4,757 and a maximum of 4,995 ABOA SF of contiguous office space within
   the Area of Consideration.
2. **Contiguous Single Floor:** Offered space must be contiguous on a single floor.
3. **Building Quality:** Space shall be located in a
   modern quality building of sound and substantial construction with a
   facade of stone, marble, brick, stainless steel, aluminum, or other
   permanent materials in good condition acceptable to the LCO. If not new,
   the building must have undergone or will complete modernization or
   adaptive reuse by occupancy.
4. **Elevator:** If offered space is above the ground floor, the building must be equipped with an elevator.
5. **Parking:** 10 structured/inside or surface/outside
   parking spaces reserved for the exclusive use of the Government, secured
   and lit in accordance with Security Requirements. Offeror must be
   willing to install fencing to secure parking spaces. Cost must be
   included in the rental consideration.
6. **Setback from Certain Uses:** Buildings must not be
   located within 1,000 linear feet of daycares, mental health or drug
   rehab clinics, immigration offices, probation and public defender's
   offices, or social services offices.
7. **Neighborhood Character (Outside City Center):**
   Space must be located (1) in an office, research, technology, or
   business park that is modern in design with a campus-like atmosphere; or
   (2) on an attractively landscaped site containing one or more modern
   office buildings that are professional and prestigious in appearance
   with surrounding development well maintained and in consonance with a
   professional image.
8. **Parking Ratio:** On-site parking must at least meet current local code requirements, or at minimum 1 space for every 250 RSF of space.
9. **Sidewalk Connectivity:** Employee and visitor entrances of the building must be connected to public sidewalks by continuous, accessible sidewalks.
10. **Nearby Amenities:** A variety of employee services
    (restaurants, retail shops, cleaners, banks) must be located within the
    immediate vicinity, accessible by continuous sidewalks, walkways, or
    pedestrian crosswalks, existing or demonstrably available by occupancy
    date.
11. **Building Shell:** Must be designed, constructed,
    and maintained in accordance with Lease Section 3 standards and
    completed prior to acceptance of space. Base structure and enclosure
    must be complete; all common areas (lobbies, fire egress corridors,
    stairwells, elevators, garages, service areas) must be complete;
    restrooms must be complete and operational; HVAC, electrical, ceilings,
    and sprinklers must be furnished, installed, and coordinated.
12. **Fully Serviced Lease:** Rent must include all
    services: utilities, HVAC during normal hours, janitorial services,
    building maintenance and system testing, landscaping and landscape
    maintenance, snow removal (if applicable), recycling, restroom supplies,
    and onsite Lessor management.
13. **Fire Protection & Life Safety:** Building must
    comply with applicable fire protection and life safety codes, including
    automatic fire sprinkler system and fire alarm system. Offeror must
    submit a Fire Protection and Life Safety Evaluation (GSA Form 12000).
14. **Accessibility:** Building must comply with the Architectural Barriers Act Accessibility Standard (ABAAS).
15. **Energy Independence and Security Act:** Building
    must comply with EISA requirements; for new construction or major
    renovation, fossil fuel-generated energy consumption must be metered and
    reported.
16. **Floodplain:** Space offered in a 100-year
    floodplain will be considered for award only if no practicable
    alternative is available. Offeror must identify whether the property is
    in a floodplain.
17. **Asbestos:** If asbestos is present, Offeror must identify its location and type and certify compliance with all applicable regulations.
18. **Environmental Due Diligence (NEPA):** Lessor must
    comply with National Environmental Policy Act requirements including
    environmental site assessments and remediation as needed.
19. **National Historic Preservation Act:** For buildings 45+ years old or in historic districts, Lessor must comply with NHPA Section 106 review.
20. **Tenant Improvements (TI):** Lessor must design and
    build TIs per final design developed after award, based on the Agency's
    full requirements. Lessor prepares Design Intent Drawings (DIDs);
    Government reviews and approves before buildout proceeds. TI allowance
    must be stated in the Lease.
21. **Building Specific Amortized Capital (BSAC) / Security:**
    Lessor must design, install, and maintain security countermeasures at
    Facility Security Level II, including employee access control, video
    surveillance system, intrusion detection system, duress alarms, key
    control program, identity verification, secured parking, cybersecurity
    for building systems, and a Facility Security Committee.
22. **Roof Rights:** If required, Government may use part of the building roof for antenna installation, included in rental consideration.
23. **Randolph-Sheppard Act:** Offeror must provide necessary utilities and related alterations if the Government places vending machines in the leased space.
24. **Offeror Name Restriction:** Offeror may not use Federal agency names or acronyms (e.g., GSA) in the entity name that owns/leases the space to GSA.
25. **Government Project Management System:** Lessor must use GSA's designated project management system for construction-phase activities.
26. **Occupant Emergency Plans:** Lessor must coordinate with GSA on Occupant Emergency Plans and participate in the Facility Security Committee.
27. **Identity Verification:** Lessor must verify the identity of all Lessor personnel and contractors accessing the building.
28. **CUI Safeguarding:** Lessor must safeguard and control dissemination of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) related to building information.
29. **Indoor Air Quality & Radon:** Lessor must maintain indoor air quality per the Lease and address radon if detected above EPA action levels.
30. **Mold & Water Intrusion:** Lessor must promptly address any mold or water intrusion issues.
31. **Liquidated Damages:** Failure to deliver space by the agreed completion date subjects Lessor to liquidated damages.

### Desirable / Conditional (Should / May)

* **Roof Antenna Space:** The Government **may** require use of part of the building roof for antenna installation. Specifications are provided separately if applicable.
* **Vending Machines:** The Government **may** provide vending machines under the Randolph-Sheppard Act, controlling the number, kind, and locations.
* **Offeror Deviations:** Offeror **may** request deviations from RLP requirements, documented on GSA Form 1364. GSA, at its sole discretion, decides whether to accept.
* **Rent Concessions:** Offeror **may** offer rent concessions (e.g., free rent).
* **Amortization Beyond Firm Term:** Offeror **may**
  elect to amortize TI costs beyond the Firm Term, but the Government
  will not be liable for any unamortized TI costs if the lease terminates
  after the Firm Term.

### Performance Metrics / SLAs

* **Space Delivery:** Lessor must complete the space
  and obtain a Certificate of Occupancy by the date established in the
  post-award schedule. Liquidated damages apply for late delivery.
* **HVAC:** Must maintain standard temperature ranges
  during normal business hours. Overtime HVAC available at the hourly rate
  offered. Evaluation assumes 80 hours of overtime HVAC per year.
* **Janitorial:** Performed per the schedule of
  periodic services in the Lease, using environmentally preferable
  cleaning products compliant with GSA standards.
* **System Maintenance & Testing:** All building
  systems (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire alarm, sprinkler, elevators,
  security systems) must be maintained and tested per manufacturer
  recommendations and Lease requirements.
* **Security Systems:** Video surveillance, intrusion
  detection, duress alarms, and access control systems must be operational
  and maintained per FSL II requirements, designed and installed by the
  Lessor with Government approval.
* **Rent Adjustment:** Operating costs and real estate
  taxes are subject to annual adjustment per the Lease terms. CPI-based
  adjustments are evaluated at 2.5% compounded annually, discounted at 5%.
* **Acceptance of Space:** Government will inspect and
  accept the space before lease commencement; TI and BSAC rent are
  withheld until completion and acceptance.
* **Overtime HVAC (Evaluation):** Estimated at 80 hours per year, discounted at 5% for present value price evaluation.
