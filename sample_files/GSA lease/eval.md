## Evaluation Criteria — GSA RLP No. 6FL0489-3

### Award Methodology

**Lowest Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA).** Section 4.03, “Award Basis,” explicitly states: *“The
 Lease will be awarded to the responsible Offeror whose offer conforms
to the requirements of this RLP and the Lease documents and is the
lowest priced technically acceptable offer submitted.”* There is no
trade-off between price and non-price factors; technical acceptability
is a pass/fail gate, and award goes to the lowest-priced offer that
passes all gates.

### Price Evaluation Detail (Section 4.04)

The Government will perform a **Present Value Price Evaluation** to determine the lowest-priced offer among technically acceptable proposals:

* Prices per ABOA SF are reduced to a composite annual ABOA SF price.
* Discount rate:  **5% annually** .
* Operating expenses escalated at  **2.5% compounded annually** , then discounted at 5%.
* Free rent is evaluated in the year offered.
* Commission credits subtracted from the first year's gross annual rent.
* Cost components added to the present value calculation include:
  * Government-provided services (if not fully serviced)
  * Lump-sum reimbursable items (not discounted)
  * Parking for Government vehicles (discounted at 5%)
  * Relocation of furniture, telecommunications, replication, and other move-related costs
  * A/E design fees and Lessor's Project Management fees (evaluated as percentage uplift on TI Allowance + BSAC amortization)
  * Overtime HVAC: based on offered hourly rate and estimated **80 hours/year** (discounted at 5%)
* All **required option periods** are included in the evaluation.
* Final metric:  **Present Value Cost per ABOA SF** .

### Additional Evaluation Mechanics

* **Competitive Range (4.01):** The LCO may establish a
  competitive range based on cost/price and other factors stated in the
  RLP. All offerors in the competitive range are given the opportunity to
  submit revised proposals. Those excluded are notified in writing.
* **Negotiations (4.01):** The LCO or designee may
  conduct oral or written negotiations with all offerors in the
  competitive range. Negotiations may address rental price for initial and
  renewal terms, and any other aspect of the offer.
* **HUBZone Price Evaluation Preference (4.02):** A
  HUBZone SBC offeror may receive a price evaluation preference under FAR
  52.219-4, as listed in the GSA 3517 General Clauses. The HUBZone offeror
  may elect to waive this preference on GSA Form 1364.
* **Submission: **All offers must be submitted
  electronically via RSAP (leasing.gsa.gov) by August 10, 2026, 5:00 PM
  Eastern. Paper submissions will not be considered. (3.02)
