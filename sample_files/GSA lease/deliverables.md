
## Deliverables & Timeline

### Submission Deadline

The RLP cover page states: **"Offers due by 8/10/2026"** with the instruction: *"In
 order to be considered for award, offers conforming to the requirements
 of the RLP must be received no later than 5:00 PM Eastern Time on the
date above."*

Section 3.02 (Receipt of Lease Proposals) confirms verbatim: **"offers
 conforming to the requirements of the RLP must be submitted through
RSAP no later than AUGUST 10, 2026, 5:00 P.M. Eastern Time."**

Offers must be submitted electronically through the Requirement Specific Acquisition Platform (RSAP) at  *leasing.gsa.gov* . Paper submissions will not be considered unless otherwise authorized by the LCO.

### Period of Performance

**Lease Term:** 15 Years, 10 Years Firm (RLP Section
1.02.F). The Government has termination rights, in whole or in parts,
effective at any time after the Firm Term by providing not less than  **90 days' prior written notice** .

The Lease Term Commencement Date is event-driven — it begins upon
acceptance of the Premises as required by the Lease (Lease Section C).
The commencement date and reconciliation of annual rent are memorialized
 by Lease Amendment (Lease Section 4.06).

**No fixed occupancy date is stated.** Occupancy is tied
 to the post-award design and construction schedule (see milestones
below). The Government will not be required to accept space prior to the
 schedule outlined in the Lease (Lease Section 4.05.C).
