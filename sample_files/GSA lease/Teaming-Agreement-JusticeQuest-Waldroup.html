RECITALS

WHEREAS, JQ is registered in the U.S. General Services Administration’s System for Award Management (“SAM.gov”) and holds an active Unique Entity Identifier (“UEI”) and CAGE Code, and provides government contracting research, compliance, and proposal administration services, including in connection with federal real property leases procured under GSA Acquisition Regulation (“GSAR”) Part 570;

WHEREAS, Broker is not independently registered in SAM.gov and holds no UEI or CAGE Code of his own;

WHEREAS, Broker is a real estate professional with expertise in identifying, evaluating, and negotiating for commercial real property;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to collaborate on identifying and pursuing opportunities for the United States Government (primarily through GSA Requests for Lease Proposals, “RLPs”) to lease real property, with JQ acting as the sole SAM-registered entity of record for all government-facing activity and Broker acting as an independent contractor providing real estate sourcing and negotiation services;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the Parties agree as follows.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 “Opportunity” means any identified federal (or, where mutually agreed in writing, state/local) government real property leasing requirement that the Parties agree to jointly pursue.

1.2 “Opportunity Addendum” means a signed addendum in the form of Exhibit C, executed for a specific Opportunity, which incorporates this Agreement by reference and sets deal-specific terms.

1.3 “Commission” means any brokerage commission payable in connection with an Opportunity, whether paid by a property owner, a cooperating broker arrangement, or otherwise.

1.4 “Commission Credit” has the meaning given in the applicable GSA lease’s “Broker Commission and Commission Credit” clause — the portion of Commission the broker of record elects to forgo, applied as a rent abatement to the Government rather than paid out.

1.5 “Property Owner” means the fee owner (or authorized representative) of real property being offered in response to an Opportunity.

1.6 “Confidential Information” has the meaning given in Article 8.

ARTICLE 2 — RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES

2.1 No joint registration. The Parties are independent contractors to one another. This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship under law, and neither Party is authorized to bind the other except as expressly set forth herein or in a signed Opportunity Addendum.

2.2 JQ as sole registrant. Because Broker is not SAM-registered, JQ is and shall remain the sole Party authorized to (a) hold itself out to any federal agency as a registered SAM.gov entity, (b) submit or facilitate submission of any offer, proposal, or communication through SAM.gov, the Requirement Specific Acquisition Platform (“RSAP”), or any other government procurement system, and (c) receive payment directly from the Government under any resulting lease, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing for a specific Opportunity.

2.3 No obligation to pursue. Nothing herein obligates either Party to pursue, or continue pursuing, any particular Opportunity. Obligations specific to a given Opportunity arise only upon execution of an Opportunity Addendum.

ARTICLE 3 — ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

The Parties’ respective roles are set forth in Exhibit A. In general: JQ is responsible for opportunity sourcing from government systems, requirements/compliance analysis, proposal drafting, and submission administration. Broker is responsible for property sourcing, owner outreach, technical site qualification, and price/terms negotiation with Property Owners. Roles for a specific Opportunity may be refined in the applicable Opportunity Addendum.

ARTICLE 4 — GOVERNMENT-FACING AUTHORITY; NO INDEPENDENT REPRESENTATIONS

4.1 Broker shall not represent to any federal employee, contracting officer, GSA broker, or property owner that Broker is registered in SAM.gov, holds a CAGE Code, or is otherwise independently eligible to be a government contract awardee.

4.2 All communications, submissions, and representations made to GSA or any other federal agency in connection with an Opportunity shall be made by, or with the prior written approval of, JQ.

4.3 Nothing in this Article authorizes JQ or Broker to sign or submit an offer on behalf of a Property Owner. Authority to act for a Property Owner must be separately documented (e.g., an exclusive listing/representation agreement between Broker and the Property Owner) before any offer naming that Property Owner is submitted.

ARTICLE 5 — EXCLUSIVITY; NON-CIRCUMVENTION; NON-SOLICITATION

5.1 Non-circumvention. Once a Party has disclosed an Opportunity or a candidate Property Owner/property to the other Party under this Agreement, neither Party shall pursue that Opportunity or contact that Property Owner independently, or through a third party, without the other Party’s written consent, for the duration of this Agreement and for [12] months thereafter.

5.2 No poaching. Neither Party shall solicit for hire or engagement any employee, contractor, or agent of the other Party introduced in connection with this Agreement, for the Term and [12] months thereafter.

5.3 Government contacts. Neither Party shall contact GSA’s broker(s) of record, contracting officer, or lease contracting officer regarding an Opportunity outside of coordinated, jointly-approved communications, except as required by law or the applicable solicitation.

ARTICLE 6 — COMPENSATION

6.1 Framework. This Agreement establishes the compensation mechanism. The default split is set forth in Exhibit B and applies to every Opportunity unless the Opportunity Addendum (Exhibit C) expressly states a different split.

6.2 Source of payment. Commission is typically paid by, or credited against, the transaction between the Property Owner/Lessor and the broker of record — not paid directly by the Government to JQ or Broker as a procurement contract line item. Compensation to JQ shall be structured as a success-fee share of Broker’s Net Commission, payable at lease execution.

6.3 No compensation for unawarded Opportunities. Except as otherwise agreed in writing, no compensation is owed under this Agreement for an Opportunity that does not result in an executed lease.

6.4 Invoicing and payment timing. The Parties acknowledge first rent payment under a federal lease is typically not received by a Lessor until approximately 45–60 days after the lease’s rent commencement date, and shall structure payment timing to trigger at lease execution rather than first rent receipt.

6.5 Expenses. Unless otherwise agreed in an Opportunity Addendum, each Party bears its own costs and expenses incurred in pursuing an Opportunity.

ARTICLE 7 — TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for [ONE (1) YEAR], automatically renewing for successive [ONE (1) YEAR] periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least [30] days before the end of the then-current term.

7.2 Termination for convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon [30] days’ written notice.

7.5 Survival of terms. Articles 5, 6.3 (as applied to pre-termination Opportunities), 8, 9, 10, and 11 survive termination.

Exhibit B — Compensation & Fee Schedule

Default structure (applies to every Opportunity unless the Opportunity Addendum expressly states a different split):

1. Trigger event: Compensation to JQ becomes due upon lease execution — not upon rent commencement or first rent receipt.

2. Base calculation — 60/40 split of Net Commission. Broker receives sixty percent (60%) of Broker’s Net Commission on the transaction; JQ receives the remaining forty percent (40%).

  • “Net Commission” means the total brokerage commission actually paid to Broker by the Property Owner (and/or received via a cooperating-broker split with any GSA broker of record), after subtracting any portion Broker voluntarily forgoes as a Commission Credit.
  • Rationale: Neither Party can close a deal without the other — JQ’s SAM.gov eligibility is required to submit any offer; Broker’s market access and negotiation are required to source and close any property. The 60/40 split reflects Broker’s deal-specific sourcing and negotiation effort while still compensating JQ for compliance packaging, proposal drafting, submission administration, and post-award administration.

3. Commission source. Broker’s Commission is paid under a separate agreement between Broker and the Property Owner. JQ has no direct claim against the Property Owner or the Government — JQ’s compensation right runs solely against Broker.

4. Payment mechanics. Broker shall remit JQ’s 40% share within ten (10) business days of Broker’s receipt of Commission. JQ does not share in the value of any credited/forgone portion of Commission.

5. No double-dipping. Separate compensation to JQ from a Property Owner or third party for the same Opportunity offsets JQ’s 40% share, unless the Addendum states both are payable.

6. Reimbursable expenses. Not shared by default; each Party bears its own costs unless an Opportunity Addendum provides otherwise.

7. Illustrative example (non-binding): Broker negotiates a $60,000 Commission and forgoes $10,000 as a Commission Credit. Net Commission is $50,000. JQ receives $20,000 (40%) within 10 business days; Broker retains $30,000 (60%).

8. Deviation. The 60/40 default may be adjusted per Opportunity in Exhibit C.
