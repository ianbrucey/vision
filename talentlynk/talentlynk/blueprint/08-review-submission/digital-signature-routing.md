# Digital Signature Routing

## Purpose
Route proposal packages and agreements to the appropriate parties for mandatory digital signature before submission. This is the legally critical step that transforms a draft into a binding submission — the vendor's sign-off on the final proposal package is not optional; it's a FAR requirement.

## What Gets Signed, By Whom

| Document | Signatory | When |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Master MOU / NDA | Vendor | Onboarding |
| Contingent Offer Letter | Specialist | Onboarding |
| Letter of Commitment | Specialist | When matched to bid |
| Task-Specific Teaming Agreement | Vendor | After pricing locked, before proposal submitted |
| Final Proposal Package (review acknowledgment) | Vendor | Before submission to government |
| Subcontract Agreement | Vendor + TalentNyk | After contract award |
| Assignment of Claims | TalentNyk + CO | Post-award, when financing needed |
| Government Forms (SF-1449, etc.) | TalentNyk authorized signatory | At submission |

## Routing Workflow

### For Each Document That Needs Signature:

1. **Generation:** Document is generated by [[../03-agreements/agreement-generation-engine]] or [[../07-proposal-generation/multi-volume-assembler]]
2. **Routing:** System determines required signatories based on document type
3. **Notification:** Email/SMS to signatory: "You have a document to review and sign: [Document Name] for Solicitation [X]"
4. **Review Portal:** Signatory clicks secure link, reviews document in browser
5. **Signature:** Signatory applies digital signature (DocuSign or equivalent)
6. **Counter-Signature:** If required, next signatory is notified
7. **Completion:** All signatures collected → document status = "Fully Executed"
8. **Storage:** Signed document stored in document repository; status updated in database

## Escalation & Reminders

- **Initial notification:** Sent immediately upon routing
- **Reminder 1:** 48 hours after no action
- **Reminder 2:** 24 hours before proposal due date (critical — signed TA is required)
- **Escalation:** Proposal Manager alerted if vendor hasn't signed within X days
- **Deadline lock:** System can be configured to WARN if proposal is within 4 hours of due date and TA is unsigned

## Signature Compliance

### ESIGN Act & UETA
- Digital signatures must be legally binding under the ESIGN Act
- Signature must be attributable to the signatory
- Audit trail: who signed, when, from what IP, what document version

### Document Immutability
- Once signed, document is locked — no further edits
- If changes needed: new version created, re-routed for signature
- Version history maintained

## Key Rules & Compliance
- FAR 15.408: Vendor must review and sign the proposal naming them before submission
- ESIGN Act (15 U.S.C. § 7001): Digital signatures are legally valid
- Signature must be from an authorized representative of the vendor company
- Unsigned Task-Specific TA at submission = regulatory violation
